Getting started

Welcome to RPGLoot, a classless Minecraft RPG. Build your character through skills, professions, equipment, talents and abilities. You can mix combat and gathering builds rather than choose a permanent class.

  1. Open your Character Book, or use /rpg handbook.
  2. Explore your character profile, skills and professions.
  3. Choose a profession suited to your preferred activity.
  4. Gather materials, earn XP and spend your points.
  5. Equip quality gear and assign abilities to empty hotbar slots.

Java and Bedrock players use normal inventory clicks. This guide describes development build 8.3.14.13; server settings may differ and new features await server testing.

Progression & talents

Skills progress through gameplay independently of your active profession. Professions award their own XP only while active. Skill and profession levels unlock talents and abilities.

PointsDefault earning ruleUse
AP1 per 5 combined skill levelsPermanent attributes
SP1 per skill level, +2 every fifthThat skill’s talents
PP1 per profession level, +3 every tenthThat profession’s talents

Open the book, select Attributes or a skill/profession talent tree, review requirements and confirm purchases. Attribute allocation supports bulk adjustments; respec refunds are available through the relevant interface. Profession talents stop contributing when you leave the profession. XP bars can be toggled with /rpg xpbars. Level caps depend on configured XP curves.

Use /rpg stats to inspect effective stats and sources. Gear, permanent allocation and active talents can contribute. Temporary buffs and equipment cannot satisfy permanent equipment requirements. Actual coefficients and caps are configurable. Health may display as one row of hearts even when effective maximum health is higher.

Weapons, armor, shields and tools have different stat pools. Registered custom gear may have permanent attribute, skill or active profession requirements. Ineligible gear cannot grant RPG bonuses. Equipment sets grant configured bonuses at piece thresholds; eligible reduction items can reduce required pieces by one, to a minimum of two. Multiple sets may be combined.

Accessories

Open Equipment → Accessories or /rpg accessories. Click an accessory in your inventory to equip it into a free matching slot. Click an equipped accessory to return it to an empty inventory slot. Two rings, amulet, earrings, belt and cloak are supported. Use an empty cursor; no dragging or shift-click. Accessories persist separately from ordinary inventory and through death. Elytra-based RPG cloaks are intended to permit gliding with a chestplate; paper cloaks do not. Restart and cross-platform behavior still need testing.

Crafting & material affinities

Quality and affinity are separate. Ordinary Rare iron can make Rare equipment with random eligible stats. Rare iron of Strength contributes a guaranteed attribute. Random stats are hidden until a successful craft, preventing free preview rerolls.

Example: two Rare Strength ingots and one Rare Agility stick yield a Rare iron sword with +8 STR and +4 AGI under bundled defaults. Mixed plain and affinity materials retain explicit inherited attributes without random filler. Use normal result-slot pickup with an empty cursor; enhanced shift-click and hotbar shortcuts are blocked. Ordinary shift-click was reported failing and deliberately left unchanged for now.

Smelting preserves quality and affinity; compatible materials stack, different affinities remain separate. Wood processing conserves total potential across outputs. Weighted rarity, progressive equipment recipes and crafter-selected stat retention are planned Forge features.

Gathering & refining

Mining

Mine eligible natural ores or supported mine blocks. Fortune preserves total yield, with at most one quality resource per original break. Silk Touch does not immediately roll quality; placed ore protection prevents repeated discovery. Use /rpg chances mining to view your chances.

Farming

Harvest mature crops to earn Farming XP and discover quality produce. Existing special seeds are distinct from the upcoming genetics engine. Raw quality food restores normal hunger without RPG ingredient buffs. Use /rpg chances farming.

Smelting

Refine quality raw ore in a furnace or blast furnace. Existing quality should not downgrade, and affinity and potential should survive. Ordinary ore may upgrade depending on configured chances and progression. Incompatible output can pause production; clear the output slot rather than mixing different qualities. Use /rpg chances smelting.

Fishing, forestry and livestock

Fishing, woodcutting and eligible breeding, taming and shearing grant skill XP. Natural quality tree genetics and animal care are not yet implemented. Ordinary vanilla trees and livestock remain usable.

Combat, mana & abilities

Open Character Book → Abilities. Choose an empty hotbar slot and an unlocked ability. Three loadouts are supported. By default, sneak and select the assigned slot to cast while retaining your weapon. Occupied slots suspend casting; mana, cooldown and requirements are checked. Use normal selection for ordinary inventory slots.

Enchanting & research

Enchanting is a profession-driven system. Join Enchanter and level the required skill before applying enchantments. Vanilla tables, books and supported EcoEnchants are subject to configured gates; ordinary repair and renaming are not intended to require Enchanter.

Visit a registered Enchanting Workshop or use /rpg workshop for its entry point. /rpg handbook catalog opens the guide. Research eligible discovered enchanted books, then manufacture or directly apply available enchantments using the displayed gold, materials and level requirements. Higher rarity and level may require more resources. The interface shows actual requirements before committing.

Mending and Repairing have special restrictions: no ordinary villager acquisition, event-exclusive restoration scroll for research and restoration essence for production. Qualified application of existing books remains possible. Keep EcoEnchants installed; the new native registry does not execute new enchantments yet.

Discovery Journal & loot

Open the Discovery Journal from the character book. Picking up quality items in Survival records material, rarity and affinity. Entries persist, are paginated and capped at 2,048 distinct entries. It records collection, not proof of original harvest or crafting. No collection rewards yet.

Quality loot can come from configured mobs, bosses, treasure and gathering. Custom named gear, accessories, sets and enchanting materials depend on server loot tables. Godly is boss-exclusive; event-exclusive materials do not drop from ordinary loot. The server uses LodeEntities for custom encounters, with optional EcoMobs support.

Economy & trading

Trade materials and gear with other players and use the server’s existing shops and currency. Affinity materials are useful for targeted builds; ordinary quality materials offer random equipment. Professions create demand for other players’ products.

The new market ledger records Furniture Workshop production. It does not yet change shop prices, track all inventory or move currency. Dynamic prices, market graphs, contracts and supply shocks are planned. A future crop disease could reduce feed supply and therefore livestock output, but this is not active gameplay today.

Troubleshooting

Why does my craft show no stats?

Random quality equipment reveals stats only after a successful normal pickup. Affinity previews show inherited attributes.

Why can’t I use an item?

Check its permanent attribute, skill and profession requirements. Check your effective stats in the profile.

Why is my station not working?

It must be registered, enabled and accessible. Some station types only have placeholder interfaces. Ask staff to inspect the block.

Why does my quality food not give buffs?

Raw ingredients no longer grant RPG buffs. Finished Chef meals are planned.

Bedrock menus

Use normal taps, empty cursor and empty inventory space. Native forms are not available yet. Report unexpected menu closures or missing items to staff.

Staff & administration

Staff reference. Commands require appropriate permissions. Back up plugin data and worlds before upgrading. Do not publish live databases, credentials or player records.

Workstations

Use /rpgstation list, look at a block within five blocks, then /rpgstation inspect and /rpgstation register water_reservoir (example). Other IDs: furniture, feed_trough, fertilizer_distributor, irrigation, forge, seed_research, sawmill, breeding, cooking. /rpgstation owner <online player> transfers ownership; /rpgstation remove removes a record subject to checks. Requires rpgloot.stations.admin.

Administration GUI

/rpgadmin requires rpgloot.admin. The station editor additionally requires station admin permission. Hold a plain material before opening the editor; select an existing recipe, assign input/output and adjust quantities. Changes save to stations.yml with a first-edit backup. Diagnostics and effect testing are available from the GUI.

Other administration

/rpg itemeditor, /rpg registry, /rpg equipmentaudit, /rpg debug mining, /rpg debug workshop, /rpg reload. Item generation and quality changes require staff permissions; never introduce test items into the player economy. Profession slot permission: rpgloot.jobs.limit.N.

Data and configuration

Back up SQLite registries and progression, player data (accessories, discoveries and loadouts), world chunks (stations), and YAML. Key files include qualities.yml, dynamic-xp.yml, stations.yml, workshop.yml, drops.yml, equipment-sets.yml, hotbar.yml and modular skills/jobs/talents. market-events.db is a new production ledger; writes are synchronous. Keep existing EcoEnchants and other required integrations until replacements are tested. Build with Maven clean package, stop server and replace only the JAR. Test registration, GUI permissions, production, persistence, crafting, smelting and Bedrock navigation before public deployment.
